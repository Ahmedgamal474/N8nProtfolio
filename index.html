How It Works (6 Automated Workflows)

  1. Recruitment 1 — Intake: Validates PDF CVs, checks for duplicate applications, uploads CVs to Google Drive, and logs candidates into Google Sheets.
  2. Recruitment 2 — AI Screening: Runs hourly to evaluate new applications against job criteria using LLMs, updates scores, and emails interview invites or declines.
  3. Recruitment 3 — Error Handler: Monitors all workflows, logs runtime errors to Google Sheets, and sends instant email alerts.
  4. Policy Loader: Syncs HR policy documents from Google Drive into the vector store automatically on startup and every morning at 7:00 AM.
  5. RAG HR Assistant: Answers employee questions strictly grounded in the loaded HR policy documents.
  6. Job Application Coach: Analyzes a candidate's CV against a specific job description and emails actionable feedback.

How It Works (2 Integrated Workflows)

  1. DawaaResv — Request & Reservation Engine:
    • Receives text messages or prescription photos via WhatsApp Trigger and parses them using Gemini Vision / LLM.
    • Matches requested items against the Google Sheets Catalog using Levenshtein fuzzy matching and suggests active-ingredient alternatives if out of stock.
    • Auto-reserves available OTC items for 2 hours and deducts inventory, or routes controlled/unclear items to the pharmacist on Telegram for one-click approval.
  2. Dawaa2Error — Back-Office & Error Handler:
    • 15-Minute Expiry Sweep: Automatically expires uncollected reservations past their 2-hour hold and restores quantities back to shelf stock.
    • Nightly Reorder Digest (22:00): Scans low-stock items against reorder thresholds and sends a consolidated restock list via Telegram and Gmail.
    • Error Classifier: Catches runtime failures, classifies them (network, validation, AI output, config), logs them to Google Sheets, and alerts the team.
